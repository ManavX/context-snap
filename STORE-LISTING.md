## Name
ContextSnap

## Short Description (132 char max)
Save and restore your full browser state — tabs, scroll positions, form inputs, and text selections — in one click.

## Detailed Description

**Never lose your browser context again.**

ContextSnap saves your entire browser state and restores it exactly where you left off. Not just tabs — your scroll positions, filled-in forms, selected text, and even pinned tabs are all preserved.

**What gets saved:**
- All open tabs (URLs, titles, pinned state)
- Scroll positions on every page
- Form inputs — text fields, dropdowns, checkboxes, radio buttons
- Text selections
- Active tab state

**What makes it different:**
- Progressive scroll for infinite-scroll pages (YouTube, Twitter, Reddit) — automatically scrolls down to reload lazy content before restoring your position
- Smart form restore that works with React, Vue, and Angular apps
- Works on job application forms (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever)
- Handles dynamic IDs and single-page apps

**Full control with settings:**
- Restore scroll position — or always start at the top
- Progressive scroll for infinite-scroll pages
- Restore form data on/off
- Restore text selections on/off
- Open restored tabs in a new window (keeps your current tabs safe)
- Max saved contexts limit
- Auto-delete old contexts after X days
- Toast notifications on/off

**Keyboard shortcuts:**
- Ctrl+Shift+S (Cmd+Shift+S on Mac) — quick save
- Ctrl+Shift+A (Cmd+Shift+A on Mac) — open context list

**Privacy first:**
- All data stays on your device (chrome.storage.local)
- No accounts, no servers, no tracking
- No data leaves your browser

Perfect for researchers, developers, job seekers, and anyone who works with many tabs and wants to pick up exactly where they left off.
